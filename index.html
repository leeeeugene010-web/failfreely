Why this exists

I failed a round of interviews. A few days later I met friends I hadn't seen in a while.

They looked happy. One of them was telling me something and I couldn't hold on to a single sentence of it. I kept nodding in the wrong places.

What hurt wasn't that they were doing well. It was that they had been through the same kind of rejections I had, and they were still applying to things anyway. They had kept moving. I had stopped. Sitting at that table, I could feel exactly how still I was.

So I built this instead of staying still.

I don't think failing is good. I think it's the receipt for having tried, and I wanted somewhere the receipts pile up where I can see them. Every entry here is a day I did something instead of nothing.

How it works

  • Log anything you went for. A job application counts. So does asking a stranger for directions.
  • Score two things. Courage — how scary it was going in. Impact — how much it stung afterward.
  • If the result comes later, the entry stays open. The day you finally open that email gets logged as its own act of courage, because not opening it is its own kind of stuck.
  • Once a week you see the gap between what you feared and what it actually cost.

What it deliberately doesn't do

  • No success rate. Nowhere in it.
  • No streaks, no flames, no "you broke your chain".
  • No leaderboard and nobody else's numbers beside yours.
  • An empty day isn't a miss. It's a slot that's still open.
